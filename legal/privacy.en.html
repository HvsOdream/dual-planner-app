DUAL Planner

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

This is an English translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version applies.

DUAL Planner ("the app") collects only what it needs to work as a planner. It carries no advertising, and we do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

1. What we collect

When you sign inThe account identifier, email address and name provided by Apple or Google (Apple provides the name only at first sign-in, and a relay address if you choose to hide your email)
While you use the appWhat you write in your planner: to-dos, daily records, one-line journal, roles, refill information, and the notes and reviews left by the assistant
If you connect a calendarThe calendar address you enter (or permission to read your device calendar) and the title, date and time of the events loaded from it. Used to show your events and find free time.
If you connect an AI assistantThe name of the connected assistant (AI agent) and the time it last read or wrote

If you use the app without signing in, your planner is still stored on the server under a random identifier created for your device. We do not collect location, contacts or advertising identifiers. The only photos we receive are the daily photo you attach yourself and the photo of a paper note you upload with "Scan paper". Note photos are used only to read the text and are not stored on the server.

2. How we use it

  • To store your planner and keep it in sync across your devices
  • To let the assistant you connected read and write your planner
  • To verify your account and answer enquiries

3. Retention and deletion

We keep your information until you delete your account. In the app, go to Menu (≡) › Account › "Delete account": your planner and account information are removed from the server immediately and cannot be recovered. Before deleting, you can download your content as a file with "Download my data".

4. Sharing

Your planner content reaches an assistant you connected yourself (for example Claude or ChatGPT) only through the connection address you gave it. Otherwise we do not provide your information to third parties, except where required by law.

5. Processors and transfers outside Korea

Cloudflare, Inc. (USA)Storage of planner content and server operation. Data is sent over the internet when you use the app, stored in Cloudflare data centres (including outside Korea), and kept until you delete your account.
Anthropic, PBC (USA)Writing the weekly review, finding roles, drafting plans, suggesting bundles, and reading paper notes. For a bundle suggestion, the titles and dates of to-dos without a plan and the titles and dates of upcoming events are sent. For a plan draft, the line you wrote, the deadline, your current situation and a few nearby to-do titles are sent; for "Scan paper", the photo of the note is sent (both only when you tap the button). For a review, that week's to-do titles, role names and hours per role are sent; for finding roles, to-do and event titles, your notes on people (for example "Minjun = son") and your one-line job description (for example "sales manager") are sent. The app asks for your consent before finding roles for the first time, and you can turn this off at any time under Menu › Where your data goes. Account information such as your name and email is not sent.
Apple Inc. · Google LLC (USA)Sign-in verification. Each company's own privacy policy also applies.
GitHub, Inc. (USA)Delivery of the app's screens. No personal information is stored.

If you do not want your data transferred abroad, you can stop using the app or delete your account. In that case, sync across devices and the assistant connection are not available.

6. Your rights

You can at any time access your information (Download my data), correct it (edit it in the app) or delete it (Delete account), and you can ask us to stop processing it. Contact us at the address below and we will act without delay.

7. How we keep it safe

  • All communication is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Sign-in tokens are verified with Apple's and Google's public keys. We never receive or store your password.
  • The assistant connection address is a random value that is hard to guess, and we advise you not to share it.

8. Children under 14

The app is intended for people aged 14 and over. If we learn that information of a child under 14 has been collected, we delete it immediately.
